# Murdoku — Solucions de tots els puzles descarregats

Aquest document recull, per a cada puzle, la graella de solucions completa
(posició de cada personatge, qui és l'assassí i qui la víctima), les claus
originals en anglès, les pistes del joc i els passos de la solució quan el
puzle els inclou.

> Coordenades: **columna (x)** i **fila (y)** comencen a 0, des de dalt-esquerra.
> El centre en píxels d'una casella és `grid_rx + x*150 + 75`, `grid_ry + y*150 + 75`.

---

(B) cannot occupy the Kitchen because if (B) were in C2, there would be no room for (A) to be northwest of him, and if (B) were in C3, (A) would have to be in the Kitchen with him, which means the Restroom would be empty.
5. The only person who can occupy the Restroom is (B). (H) cannot occupy the Restroom because she would have to be in R3, which would mean there's no room for (B) to be northwest of her. Since (B) has to be southeast of (A), this isolates (A) to R3 C2, and (B) has to be in R4.
6. This isolates (H) to the Bedroom, somewhere in R6 or R7. If (G) was in R6, (F) would have to be in R7, which would block (H). So (G) must be in R7 and (F) must be in R8. This isolates (H) to R6 C8. This isolates (B) then (D), and lastly (V) in the Living Room with (G). Grace is the murderer!
